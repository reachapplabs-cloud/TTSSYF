## How priority was assigned

Priority is not a claim about which question matters more spiritually — it is a research-sequencing judgment: P0 questions are the ones whose answers condition how several other questions should be read (e.g. resolving how much evidentiary weight "recorded in Sahih Muslim" should carry, per `METHODOLOGY.md`, changes how every other hadith-based question in this project should be approached), or that go to the root of a major doctrinal pillar (Qur'an integrity, who counts as Ahl al-Bayt, the legitimacy of the Imamate). P1 covers major supporting pillars. P2 covers specific case studies that illustrate or support a P0/P1 question. P3 is reserved for items the source material itself left too vague to research yet (e.g. §4.7, where the companion in question is never named). This ordering is a starting recommendation, not a constraint — work whatever question is most useful next.

## Recommended research order (P0 only, suggested sequence)

1. **§2.1 and §3.2** — settle the methodology proof-cases first (how much weight does "Sahih Muslim" carry when Abu Hurairah's own record is contested?), since the answers directly shape how to weigh every other hadith-citation question in this project.
2. **§1.1 and §1.2** — Qur'an integrity, the accusation this project exists partly to rebut.
3. **§5.1 and §5.2** — who is Ahl al-Bayt, and the Fadak/Fatima grievance — the emotional and doctrinal core of the Shia case.
4. **§6.1 and §6.4** — the Imamate/Ghadeer Khumm case, including independently verifying all nine cited references in §6.4.
5. **§4.2 and §4.8** — the Qirtas incident and the "blanket justice for companions" framework.
