**Note:** the claim that "all from his line can only die a death of
battle or poisoning" is the contributor's own framing (per
`METHODOLOGY.md` Principle 4, an unverified claim, not a cited source).
The "4-year dormant toxin" detail refers to the well-known account of a
Jewish woman at Khaybar poisoning a meal served to the Prophet ﷺ, after
which some Sunni sources report he said Allah had informed him of the
poison and he was protected from it at the time — the question here is
whether a delayed effect of that same poisoning is a plausible or
recorded cause of his death years later. This project has not yet
opened any of the five aimode links to see what case they make.
